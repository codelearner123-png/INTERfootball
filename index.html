How to play

Move your blue player with the on-screen arrows or W A S D. Get close to the ball, then press SHOOT (Space) to aim for the top goal. Use PASS (X) for a shorter kick. The red computer player defends and tries to score at the bottom goal.

Use KICK (C) for a firm forward touch. Choose a stadium and a fictional player from the main menu. Score goals to earn coins and build your free Pro level. Training goals earn smaller coin rewards.

In Training Ground, the opponent stays out of the way and the clock is turned off. Pause or return to the main menu whenever you like.
